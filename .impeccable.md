# .impeccable

Project design context for the **Africa Multiple — DRE** Omeka S theme.
See `DESIGN.md` for the full system reference and `AUDIT.md` for the current
design/token audit and its work order.

## Design Context

### Users
Researchers, postgraduate students, and the interested public engaging with the
digital collections and research data of the Africa Multiple Cluster of
Excellence (University of Bayreuth). They arrive with a question, not a browse
intent: the first screen has to offer a search field and an honest map of the
corpora. They settle into long reading sessions on individual records —
sometimes late at night, hence dark mode matters. International audience
(English/French, transliterated names), often on institutional or mobile
networks.

### Brand Personality
Scholarly · warm · authoritative. The voice is considered and precise but never
corporate or flashy — earthy and human. Emotional goal: confidence and calm
focus, the feeling of a well-kept research library rather than a product
dashboard. It should read as part of the Africa Multiple family alongside the
AMIRA dashboard.

### Aesthetic Direction
**"Scholarly Modernism."** Uni-Grün (`#009260`) as the signature colour over warm
stone neutrals in light mode and a Uni-Grün-tinted forest dark in dark mode.
Display serif (Spectral) paired with a humanist grotesque (Hanken Grotesk).
**Type and rule carry the page** — large display titles, hairline dividers,
tabular numerals, a 3 px brand flag — rather than decorative fills. Generous
space, soft warm shadows used sparingly, quiet brand accents.

- **References:** the AMIRA dashboard (brand + Scholarly Modernism); the
  reading-first record pages of research-library catalogues. Dark mode uses a
  `data-theme` + OKLCH-token technique.
- **Anti-references:** generic AI admin UIs — left/right accent stripes,
  gradient text, glassmorphism, neon-on-dark, cold grey neutrals, the
  Inter/DM-Sans/Fraunces monoculture. **And: stock Omeka S / Lively furniture** —
  a flat `<dl>` dump of every property in database order, a coloured rule under
  every region container, a page whose only visual idea is a coloured header
  band.
- **Theme:** light by default, dark on request (system-preference aware, manual
  toggle persists). Dark mode is an equal citizen: every new surface is designed
  in both.
- **Imagery:** none. The theme is photography-free by policy; mastheads are
  typographic. Item media (scans, IIIF canvases) is content, not decoration.

### Design Principles
1. **One seed, everything derived.** A single `--primary-base` drives every
   accent via `color-mix(in oklab, …)`; changing it re-tints the whole theme and
   stays AA-legible in both modes. Never hard-code a brand shade in a component.
2. **Tokens, not hexes — and tokens all the way down.** Components speak in
   semantic tokens (`--surface`, `--ink`, `--border`, `--primary`), and so do
   **type, spacing and layout**: sizes come from `--text-*`, rhythm from
   `--leading-*` and `--space-*`, page structure from `--container-*`,
   `--header-height`, `--label-col`. One scale per decision, defined in CSS —
   no parallel Sass number set, no off-grid px.
3. **Content before apparatus.** On a record, the title, author and abstract come
   first at a real reading measure; subjects are chips; identifiers, IDs and
   URIs are grouped last; citation, licence and viewer live in a rail. Never
   render metadata in database order.
4. **Warm, scholarly, restrained.** Earth-tone neutrals, refined type, quiet
   accents. Decoration must earn its place and reinforce the brand. Prefer a
   hairline and a numeral to a card and a shadow.
5. **Legible and accessible first.** WCAG AA pairings in both modes — *measured*,
   not asserted; always-visible focus, reduced-motion respected, real reading
   measures. `--muted` is for 15 px+ non-essential text only.
6. **The tokens are a public API.** DRE-Search and ResourceVisualizations consume
   them. Add freely; rename only with an alias and a changelog entry (see
   `AUDIT.md` §4).
7. **No AI tells, no stock-theme tells.** Match-and-refuse both anti-reference
   lists above — especially the side-stripe accent, gradient text, and the
   ungrouped metadata dump.
